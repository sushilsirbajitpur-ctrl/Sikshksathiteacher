Disclaimer: Shikshak Sathi Bihar is an independent private digital workspace created by SK Tech Web Dev to assist teachers. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official application of any government department or government entity.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: When you register, we collect your full name, 10-digit mobile number (used as your User ID), optional Gmail address (for account recovery), and school details (District, Block, Panchayat/UDISE code, and School Name).
  • User Content: Personal diary notes, optional diary photos uploaded by you, and feedback/messages sent to the admin.
  • Basic Usage Statistics: Anonymous feature usage counts (such as app visits, logins, and total GPS photo counts) to improve app performance.

2. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Camera: Used solely for the in-app GPS Camera and Document Scanner → PDF features. Photos taken with the GPS Camera and scanned documents are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Location (GPS): Used strictly on-demand inside the GPS Camera feature to stamp the approximate address, latitude, and longitude onto the photo you capture. Your location data is never stored on our servers or tracked in the background.
  • Storage / Media: Used only when you choose to upload images to your personal diary or save/share generated PDFs, Word (.docx) files, and GPS photos on your device.

3. Local Storage & Third-Party Services

Daily lesson-diary period selections and login sessions are stored locally on your device. To provide core functionality, the app uses trusted third-party services:

  • Supabase: Secure user authentication and database storage for profiles, lesson plans, question papers, notices, and personal diary notes.
  • Cloudflare Workers & R2: Secure cloud storage for images explicitly uploaded by you inside the personal diary feature.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): Used only to convert GPS coordinates into a readable place name when stamping a GPS photo.

4. Data Security & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. All network communication between the app and our backend servers is encrypted over HTTPS.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time directly inside the app by opening Change Password (🔑 पासवर्ड बदलें) → Request Account Deletion (🗑 खाता हटाने का अनुरोध), or by emailing us at the contact address below.

  • लोकेशन (GPS): केवल GPS कैमरा से फ़ोटो खींचते समय उस पर स्थान का नाम और अक्षांश-देशांतर छापने के लिए उपयोग होती है। आपकी लोकेशन हमारे सर्वर पर सेव नहीं की जाती और न ही बैकग्राउंड में ट्रैक होती है।
